# Slopfest Roadmap to 1.0

The goal for 1.0 is a complete single-player skirmish game, not merely a feature demo.

## 0.5 — Game shell and planet life
- title / pause / settings menus
- automatic planet arrival menu
- equipment shops
- distinct weapon models
- gentler and configurable early AI pressure
- stronger pixel-art presentation

## 0.6 — A galaxy of actors
- 3–5 AI factions
- faction personalities
- diplomacy states and reputation
- ceasefires, trade relations, alliances
- replacement ships / respawn at surviving shipyards
- proper invasion-force resource

## 0.7 — Working economy
- dynamic commodity demand and prices
- known-price trade computer
- route opportunities
- contracts and missions
- pirates / unaffiliated threats

## 0.8 — The old galaxy underneath
- derelicts
- artifacts
- Wayline relay sites
- unique modules
- expanded Severance fragments
- contradictory historical accounts

## 0.9 — Complete match
- match setup and galaxy seed
- multiple victory conditions
- save/load
- AI that understands the main player verbs
- balance pass
- input remapping and resolution/UI polish

## 0.95 — Shipping pass
- sound and music
- tutorial / guided first match
- accessibility and readability pass
- packaging for modern Windows
- performance profiling
- bug fixing

## 1.0
A stable, replayable single-player skirmish game with exploration, economy, ship building,
planet management, warfare, diplomacy, lore, victory conditions, saves, and polished UX.

Multiplayer remains post-1.0, but simulation/rendering architecture should not preclude it.


## Implemented through 0.8

0.6, 0.7, and 0.8 milestone systems are present. Next: 0.9 complete-match work (match setup, victory paths, save/load, AI/balance, input and resolution polish).


## Implemented through 0.9

A complete match now exists: setup, seed, multiple victory paths, save/load/autosave, and explicit victory progress. Remaining road to 1.0 is the shipping pass: audio, onboarding/tutorial, accessibility/input polish, visual cohesion, performance, balance, packaging, and ruthless bug fixing.
